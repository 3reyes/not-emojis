# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in not-emojis respectful and welcoming, regardless of background or identity. Contributors are expected to communicate professionally and treat others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against others.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. To report a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Maintainers should make a private contact route available in the repository settings.
